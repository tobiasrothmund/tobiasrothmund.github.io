In 2009 and 2010, standardised written surveys were conducted with students at eight randomly selected schools in Rhineland-Palatinate. The participating schools comprised three *Hauptschulen*, three *Realschulen* and two *Gymnasien* (the three tiers of German secondary education), located in rural areas, in a mid-sized town (approx. 100,000 inhabitants) and in a city (approx. 200,000 inhabitants).

In 2009, a total of 1,271 students from 56 classes took part in the survey. In 2010, 796 students from 39 classes were surveyed. In the end, data from both measurement points (2009 and 2010) were available for 603 individuals (75.8% of the 2010 respondents).
