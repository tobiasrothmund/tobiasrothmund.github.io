In autumn 2010, an online survey was conducted before, during and after the mediation talks on Stuttgart 21, the controversial railway and urban development project (the protest slogan *Oben bleiben* – "stay above ground" – refers to the planned underground station); a total of 1,005 people took part. The study examined to what extent experiences of injustice psychologically underpin protest behaviour against Stuttgart 21. At the same time, it assessed how far the mediation talks helped to pacify the so-called *Wutbürger* ("angry citizens").
