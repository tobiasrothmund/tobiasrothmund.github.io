The Fakt-o-Mat is an interactive online quiz in which users can test their political fact knowledge. With 24 questions on two controversial political topics, it shows how strongly one's own opinion can influence which facts one believes to be true – and helps users recognise where a fact ends and an opinion begins.

The Fakt-o-Mat was developed at the Department of Communication Science at Friedrich Schiller University Jena in cooperation with KomRex as part of the research project [SensipoV](/en/research/sensipov/) on raising awareness of political bias.
