On 26 and 27 March 2026, more than 80 psychologists – mostly researchers at different career stages and from various subdisciplines – met at the Psychologische Hochschule Berlin to discuss the self-understanding and role of psychology in socio-political discourse and decision-making.

The conference resulted in a public call setting out five key points:

1. **Responsibility:** Psychologists and the German Psychological Society (DGPs) are responsible for disseminating and applying scientific psychology for the benefit of people and society.
2. **Impact:** Psychological expertise is underrepresented in policy advisory bodies and policy-related research institutions; transfer structures should be expanded.
3. **Education:** Alongside research training, psychology degree programmes should do more to foster political engagement and communication skills and increase the diversity of students.
4. **Threats:** Growing hostility towards scientists and institutions endangers academic freedom – psychologists in Germany, too, report verbal attacks and threats.
5. **Solidarity:** When colleagues are attacked personally, the discipline needs solidarity, civil courage and support structures.

The call can be signed online.
