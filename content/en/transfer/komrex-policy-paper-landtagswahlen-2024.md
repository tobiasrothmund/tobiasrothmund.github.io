This policy paper by KomRex (University of Jena) and the Chair of Social Psychology at Trier University presents first results of the population survey on the 2024 state elections in Thuringia, Saxony and Brandenburg (see the research project [EGSES24](/en/research/egses24/)). For each of the three states, it analyses voter migration, coalition preferences, trust in politics and attitudes towards democracy.

Key findings:

1. **Shift towards the margins:** Between the 2021 federal election and the 2024 state elections, voting behaviour shifted markedly towards the ideological margins. The AfD gained mainly former CDU and FDP voters, while the BSW drew support from across the political spectrum.
2. **Coalition preferences:** The CDU is the most desired coalition partner in all three states; AfD participation in government is rejected by a majority outside the AfD's own electorate.
3. **Trust and democracy:** Around two thirds of AfD voters have little or no trust in the federal government, the state government and public broadcasting; more than a quarter reject democracy as the best form of government. BSW voters also show heightened scepticism towards democratic institutions – a challenge for forming stable governments.
