Social psychological peace and conflict research in Germany has so far been poorly networked and has little institutional anchoring. The strategy conference "Perspectives for Social Psychological Peace Research in Germany" brought experts together in Jena from 14 to 16 October 2022 to develop the field jointly.

**Aims of the conference**

- **Strengthening networks:** Researchers from social psychology and peace and conflict research were to connect more closely and build shared structures.
- **Broadening the scope:** Alongside international conflicts, extremism, discrimination and migration were to receive more attention as topics of peace research.
- **Initiating new collaborations:** Participants developed ideas for joint, interdisciplinary research projects.

Rather than a traditional conference, the event was designed as an interactive, hybrid workshop – with on-site panel discussions and digital inputs. The results were to be developed into position papers and published in a special issue.
